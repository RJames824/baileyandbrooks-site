Why I went out on my own

I spent more than ten years at Voit Real Estate Services, leasing and selling industrial buildings across North County San Diego.

Day to day, I wasn't going after institutional owners or nationwide tenants. The work I enjoyed was with people who own the building their business runs out of, and with local investors.

Opening my own brokerage is more in line with my motives and my values.

My objective is to create transparent and honest relationships throughout all transactions with the end goal of turning over keys and everyone feeling like it was a fair deal.

Money is the reason most of us get into business. Financial freedom is what most of us are after. I want that for my family, and I want it for my clients.

Good to know

Prop 19 could save you on property taxes

I used Prop 19 myself. A lot of people who qualify have never heard of it.

If you own your home in California and you are 55 or older, severely disabled, or lost your home to a wildfire or natural disaster, you may be able to take your current property tax base with you when you buy your next home.

  • Works anywhere in California, not just in your county
  • Can be used up to three times if you are 55+ or severely disabled
  • The new home can cost more. Only the difference gets taxed at today's value
  • You file the claim with the County Assessor where the new home is

Thinking about a move and wondering if this fits you? Ask me. I'll walk you through how it worked for me and point you to the right forms. Read the state's fact sheet

General information, not tax advice. Rules and deadlines apply. Confirm your situation with your County Assessor or a tax professional.
