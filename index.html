Why I went out on my own

I spent more than ten years at Voit Real Estate Services, leasing and selling industrial buildings across North County San Diego.

Day to day, I wasn't going after institutional owners or nationwide tenants. The work I enjoyed was with people who own the building their business runs out of, and with local investors.

Opening my own brokerage is more in line with my motives and my values. So I did.

Money is the reason most of us get into business. Financial freedom is what most of us are after. I want that for my family, and I want it for my clients.
